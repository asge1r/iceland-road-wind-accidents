# Traffic data validation

## Purpose

The daily PDF counts and the annual road-section statistics have different
roles. Daily counts describe observed traffic at fixed counters on individual
dates. ADU, SDU, and VDU provide annual or seasonal average daily traffic for
road sections. This check tests whether the daily PDF extraction is on the same
scale as official ADU.

## Comparison

For each counter and year, the mean observed daily PDF traffic is compared with
ADU for the same road section and year. The clean validation sample requires:

- an exact road-section and year match;
- at least 300 observed days;
- one physical counter site on the road section.

Directional channels recorded for the same station ID are summed before this
comparison. Distinct counter stations on the same road section are not summed.

## Result

The clean sample contains 1,122 counter-years. The median ratio of mean observed
daily traffic to ADU is 1.000. The correlation between their logarithms is
0.993. The close agreement supports the PDF extraction, units, directional
channel aggregation, and road-section-year linkage.

This does not make the sources interchangeable. Daily counts are used to
describe changes in travel demand during windy days in 2019-2024. ADU, SDU,
and VDU are used as wider road-section exposure measures. They are period
averages and do not measure traffic at the accident minute.
