# Supervisor figure specification

The figure families below are retained from Kristján’s specification. The
15 September presentation update adds a centred All year panel above the four
seasons and updates thesis numbering. The generic traffic-rate filenames below
use **same-day allocation** and are supporting sensitivities; the main monthly
allocation figures have the `monthly_` prefix. See
[the figure mapping](../../../docs/kristjan_readme_figure_mapping.md) for methods
and source-data checks.

- [weather_oe_2007_2018.png](weather_oe_2007_2018.png) — Whole-year observed/expected accident ratios for wind, gust and temperature in 2007–2018.
- [weather_oe_2019_2024.png](weather_oe_2019_2024.png) — The corresponding whole-year observed/expected ratios for 2019–2024, without traffic adjustment.
- [traffic_weather_response.png](traffic_weather_response.png) — Traffic relative to its usual level in each wind, gust and temperature interval, estimated from the 2019–2024 daily counters.
- [weather_oe_traffic_corrected_2019_2024.png](weather_oe_traffic_corrected_2019_2024.png) — The 2019–2024 observed/expected ratios after an approximate adjustment for the measured traffic response.
- [weather_rate_annual.png](weather_rate_annual.png) — Whole-year injury-accident rates per million estimated vehicle-km for wind, gust and temperature, using 2019–2024 daily traffic and same-day weather allocation.
- [f_traffic_rate_panels.png](f_traffic_rate_panels.png) — The corresponding mean-wind accident rates for All year and each of the four seasons, with the seasonal final bin covering ≥15 m/s.
- [fg_traffic_rate_panels.png](fg_traffic_rate_panels.png) — The corresponding gust accident rates for All year and each season, with the seasonal final bin covering ≥20 m/s.
- [temperature_traffic_rate_panels.png](temperature_traffic_rate_panels.png) — The corresponding temperature accident rates for All year and each of the four seasons.
- [wind_oe_panels.png](wind_oe_panels.png) — Whole-year and seasonal mean-wind O/E for 2007–2025, without traffic adjustment, used in thesis Figure 4.3.
- [gust_oe_panels.png](gust_oe_panels.png) — Whole-year and seasonal gust O/E for 2007–2025, without traffic adjustment, used in thesis Figure 4.4.
- [temperature_oe_panels.png](temperature_oe_panels.png) — Whole-year and seasonal temperature O/E for 2007–2025, without traffic adjustment, used in thesis Figure 4.5.
