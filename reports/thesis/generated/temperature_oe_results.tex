The all-injury O/E is 1.22 immediately below freezing, falls to 0.71 at 3--6$^{\circ}$C, and rises to 1.60 at temperatures of at least 12$^{\circ}$C. The coldest group, below $-6$$^{\circ}$C, has O/E 0.84; thus the pattern is not a uniform cold-weather excess. Seasonal warm-end components are smaller and less stable than the pooled comparison.
