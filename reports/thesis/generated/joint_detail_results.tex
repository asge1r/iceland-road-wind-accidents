Figure~\ref{fig:joint-weather-detail} uses the same five wind and eight temperature intervals as the marginal figures. The all-temperature O/E rises from 0.95 at 0--5 m/s to 1.45 at 15--20 m/s and 2.15 at 20 m/s or above. Within the 15--20 m/s row, adequately supported cells range from 0.93 at 3--6$^{\circ}$C to 2.06 immediately below freezing. The all-wind temperature margin is 1.22 immediately below freezing and 1.60 at 12$^{\circ}$C or above, but 0.71 at 3--6$^{\circ}$C.

Six of the eight cells at wind speeds of at least 20 m/s have limited support. The largest O/E (7.63) occurs at 20 m/s or above and at least 12$^{\circ}$C, but represents only 2 observed accidents versus 0.26 expected. That extreme cell should not be read as evidence of a combined wind--temperature effect.
