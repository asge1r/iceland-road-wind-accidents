\section{Supporting Allocated Daily-Counter Sample}

This section documents the supporting allocated daily-counter model,
not the 694-accident traffic-based rate analysis. The tables show how the
2019--2024 rural injury sample narrows under the linkage used by that supporting
model. The 767 records at the last broad selection step include five records
that do not enter its final allocated-rate model, which retains 762 accidents.

Table~\ref{tab:allocated-selection} traces the linkage exclusions for this
supporting sample.
